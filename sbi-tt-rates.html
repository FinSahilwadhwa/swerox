SBI TT Rates FAQ

Quick answers before filing.

Short notes for taxpayers, finance teams and preparers who need SBI TT buying rate PDFs for compliance records.

What are SBI TT rates?

SBI TT rates are Telegraphic Transfer buying and selling rates published by State Bank of India for major currencies.

Why are they used for ITR?

Foreign income may need conversion into INR for Indian tax reporting. The SBI TT buying rate is commonly checked for this purpose.

Can I view older rates?

Yes. Select an older date. The PDF path updates automatically to the archive folder for that year and month.

What if the PDF does not open?

SBI may not publish files on some holidays or weekends. Try the previous working day, or verify directly on SBI's website.
